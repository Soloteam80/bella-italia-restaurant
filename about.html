Our Story

Bella Italia was created around a simple idea: good food brings people together. Inspired by the traditions, flavours, and warmth of Italy, we create dishes made to be shared and moments made to be remembered. Come discover what makes Bella Italia feel like a little taste of Italy in Dublin.

A Little Taste of Italy, Right at Your Table.

Bella Italia was created around a simple idea: Italian food should feel generous, authentic and welcoming.

Our kitchen takes inspiration from traditional recipes from across Italy, combining familiar favourites with dishes that showcase the character of different regions.

We believe in quality ingredients, careful preparation and food made to be enjoyed together. Whether you're joining us for a quick lunch, a family dinner, a romantic evening or simply a pizza and a glass of wine, there's always a place for you at Bella Italia.

Good food. Good wine. Good company. That's our idea of Italy.
